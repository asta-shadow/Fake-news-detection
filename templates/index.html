About This Application

VeriFact AI is an intelligent Fake News Detection system designed to help users identify misinformation and verify the credibility of news articles using Artificial Intelligence and Large Language Models (LLMs).

The platform allows users to verify news using two different methods:

  • 📝 Text Analysis – Users can paste any news article text and the AI will analyze it to determine if it is likely real or fake.
  • 🔗 URL Analysis – Users can paste a news article link, and the system automatically extracts the article content and evaluates its credibility.
  • ⚡ Trending News Verification – Displays trending headlines that users can quickly analyze.

The application combines Machine Learning techniques with advanced LLM reasoning to provide explanations and insights about the reliability of the news content.

This project demonstrates how Artificial Intelligence can be used to combat misinformation and improve digital media literacy.
